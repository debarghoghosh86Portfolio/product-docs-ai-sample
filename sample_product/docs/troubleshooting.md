# Troubleshooting

## I'm not getting notification emails

1. Check **Profile > Notifications** and make sure email notifications are on.
2. Add `notifications@taskflow.example` to your contacts or safe-senders list.
3. Check your spam or promotions folder.
4. Ask your IT team whether the domain `taskflow.example` is blocked.

## The board is slow or not loading

Clear your browser cache, disable browser extensions, and reload. Projects with more than 5,000 open tasks load faster in **List** view. Check the status page at `status.taskflow.example` for outages.

## I was signed out unexpectedly

This usually happens after a password reset (which signs out all other devices), when your session expired after 30 days of inactivity, or when an admin enforced SSO.

## I can't move a task to Done

The task is probably blocked by another task. Open it and check the **Dependencies** section; finish or remove the blocking tasks first.

## Contact support

Free plan users can get help from the community forum. Pro and Business users can email `support@taskflow.example`; Business plan customers get a 4-hour response time on business days.
