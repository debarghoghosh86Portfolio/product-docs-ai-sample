# Getting started with TaskFlow

TaskFlow is a fictional task and project management app used as sample data for this repository. Teams use it to plan work, assign tasks, and track progress on boards.

## Create your account

1. Go to the TaskFlow sign-up page and enter your work email.
2. Open the verification email and click **Verify email**. The link expires after 24 hours.
3. Choose a password with at least 12 characters, including one number and one symbol.
4. Enter your workspace name. This becomes your workspace URL, for example `acme.taskflow.example`.

## Invite your team

Workspace owners and admins can invite members from **Settings > Members > Invite**. You can paste up to 50 email addresses at once. Invitations expire after 7 days; you can resend an expired invitation from the same page.

Each member gets one of three roles:

- **Owner**: full control, including billing and deleting the workspace. Every workspace has exactly one owner.
- **Admin**: manage members, projects, and integrations, but not billing.
- **Member**: create and edit tasks in projects they belong to.

## Create your first project

Click **New project** in the left sidebar, give it a name, and pick a template: *Blank*, *Kanban*, *Sprint*, or *Bug tracker*. You can change the template's columns later from **Project settings > Board**.

## System requirements

TaskFlow runs in the latest two versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. Mobile apps are available for iOS 16+ and Android 11+.
