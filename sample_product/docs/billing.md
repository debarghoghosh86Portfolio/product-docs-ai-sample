# Plans and billing

## Plans

| Plan     | Price (per user / month, billed yearly) | Highlights |
|----------|-----------------------------------------|------------|
| Free     | $0                                      | 5 projects, 10 members, 2 GB storage |
| Pro      | $8                                      | Unlimited projects, Timeline view, 100 GB storage |
| Business | $15                                     | Everything in Pro + SSO, required 2FA, audit log, 1 TB storage |

Monthly billing costs 20% more than yearly billing.

## Change your plan

Only the workspace **owner** can change plans. Go to **Settings > Billing > Change plan**. Upgrades take effect immediately and you are charged a prorated amount. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period.

## Payment methods

TaskFlow accepts Visa, Mastercard, and American Express. Business plan customers with 50 or more seats can pay by invoice.

## Refunds

Yearly plans can be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase. Monthly plans are not refundable, but you can cancel any time and keep access until the end of the month.

## Cancel your subscription

Go to **Settings > Billing > Cancel subscription**. Your workspace moves to the Free plan at the end of the billing period. If you have more than 5 projects, the extra projects become read-only.
