rough notes from eng for the webhooks how-to:
- settings > integrations > webhooks > add webhook
- need admin or owner role
- paste https URL (http not allowed), pick events: task.created task.updated task.completed project.archived
- copy the secret shown once, store it safely
- each POST has X-TaskFlow-Signature header = hex HMAC-SHA256(body, secret)
- respond 2xx within 10s otherwise counts as failed
- retries 5x exponential backoff (1m,5m,30m,2h,6h) then webhook disabled + email to admins
- "send test event" button for testing
- common probs: signature mismatch usually because body was parsed/re-serialized before hashing; use raw body. 410 Gone from your endpoint = we delete the webhook
